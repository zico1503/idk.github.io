A NOTE FROM NIM

A slow garden, a small plan.

  1. Plant: choose a seed in your pouch and tap an empty bed.
  2. Water: use one dew drop on a plant before you end the day. Rainy days water the whole patch for free.
  3. Grow: watered plants grow one stage each night. A sparkle means the flower is ready.
  4. Harvest: pick a mature flower, then deliver the neighbor’s bouquet or sell spare blooms.
  5. Discover: fill market notes to unlock new seeds. Your game saves automatically in this browser.
