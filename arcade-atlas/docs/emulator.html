How it works

Carts are Atlas-8 assembly source. A two-pass assembler in this page turns them into 32 KB ROM images, which the AV8 core executes 60 frames a second. The VDP understands a handful of commands written to its registers — clear screen, plot pixel, draw an 8×8 tile — and the keypad and beeper are memory-mapped too. No copyrighted ROMs, no plugins; everything here is original.

The three built-in carts were written for the Atlas-8 specifically for Arcade Atlas.
