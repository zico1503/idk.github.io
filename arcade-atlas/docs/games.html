The Games area

Five ways to get lost. Campaign games save every mission, star and upgrade to your profile; arcade games save your best runs. Everything runs in the browser — nothing to install.

How campaigns work

Each campaign game tracks missions, stars and unlocks in your profile automatically. Leave anytime — you resume exactly where the story left off. Estimated campaign lengths (played once, at a steady pace):

Shared controls

Esc pauses any game · M mutes · arrow keys / WASD move · Enter confirms menus. On touch devices every game shows a joystick and action buttons automatically.

Games detect slower hardware and drop effects automatically; you can also force quality from any pause menu → Settings.
