Built by hand, runs anywhere

Every world on Arcade Atlas is written from scratch — original code, original art drawn in canvas and WebGL, original music synthesized in your browser. Nothing is loaded from remote servers, so once the page is up you can play offline, and the site works on school Chromebooks as happily as on a gaming rig.

Campaign games automatically scale their effects for slower devices, and every game is playable muted with touch or keyboard.
