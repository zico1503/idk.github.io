🍌 Banana Catchers

You are the catcher of the Suncrest Grove. Bananas tumble from the canopy — catch every bunch, chain combos for big points, and keep coconuts and bees out of your basket.

Goal: finish all 12 harvest levels (each asks for a banana quota before time runs out), then chase your best score in Endless mode.

Controls

Move← → / A D · drag or touch stick
PauseEsc or P
MuteM or header button

Campaign

12 harvest levels with rising quotas, wind gusts, bee swarms and coconut rain — about 20–30 minutes to clear, then endless chasing. Stars are awarded for accuracy (3 ★ = near-perfect run).

Progress and best scores save automatically to your profile.
