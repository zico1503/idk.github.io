🌺 Thornward

The Blight crawls out of the Mire and only the old thorn-magic stands in its way. Plant living thorn-tenders along the invasion paths and protect the last Heartbloom across 5 groves.

Goal: hold every wave. Stars are earned for flawless defenses and unlock permanent Requisition perks between missions.

Campaign

25 missions over 5 groves — Sunfield, Mossy Brook, Stonehollow, Mirefen and the Nightmare Hedge. 8 tower families with 3 upgrade tiers each; armored, flying, healing and elite blights; 3 boss encounters. Estimated length: 2.5–3.5 hours including retries and star chasing.

Watch for Sporewings — they fly over walls, so keep Falconer Nests handy from Grove 3 on.
