🏮 Emberhollow

A lantern-lit action RPG. The Gloam swallowed the sun three dawns ago and only the Last Lantern still answers. You are its new Warden: relight the valley, one brazier at a time.

Goal: play through 5 chapters (20 quests including 6 side stories), defeat 4 bosses, gather charms and lantern hues, and bring the dawn back to Emberhollow.

Campaign

Ember's Wake → The Gloomwood → Mirefen Depths → Hollow Keep → Dawnrise. Braziers are checkpoints (they refill health and lantern oil). Your light radius burns with your oil — manage it. Shielded knights block frontal strikes; roll behind them. Estimated length: 2.5–4 hours including side quests and collecting 12 emberlings and 10 lore pages.

Progress autosaves at braziers and chapter breaks. Works on touch and keyboard.
