✈ Driftpost

A real-time 3D courier game rendered in WebGL. The Gale Post has openings for a rookie with good instincts: fly a drift-kite between the floating isles of the Gale, delivering post, racing ring courses, re-lighting beacon chains and threading storm cells.

Goal: fly all 24 missions across 5 regions, climb from Trainee to Postmaster of the Isles, hunt 45 skyshards and refit your kite at the post office.

Campaign

5 regions × 5 missions (24 total): a hands-on tutorial, deliveries, beacon chains, balloon hunts, ring races, storm runs, night lantern flights, multi-stop long hauls — and the Century Flight finale. Stars unlock regions and ranks; coins buy engine, wings, hull, lantern and compass upgrades plus paints and trails. 45 skyshards are hidden across the sky. Estimated length: 2.5–3.5 hours.

Needs WebGL (works on school Chromebooks — quality auto-scales on slower devices).
